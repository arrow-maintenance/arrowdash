* **Apache Arrow ADBC 24 release:** Release of ADBC 24 featuring over 55 fixes, including critical updates to Windows JNI artifacts to ensure proper static linking. [Thread](https://lists.apache.org/list?dev@arrow.apache.org:lte=2M:VOTE%20Release%20Apache%20Arrow%20ADBC%2024%20-%20RC2)

* **Swift 6.0 minimum version proposal:** Proposal to raise the minimum Swift version to 6.0 to utilize native Int128 support, a key requirement for implementing Decimal types. [Thread](https://lists.apache.org/list?dev@arrow.apache.org:lte=2M:DISCUSS%20Swift%20Proposal%20to%20raise%20minimum%20Swift%20version%20to%206.0)

* **Windows on Arm (win_arm64) support:** Microsoft and Linaro proposed a plan to provide engineering resources and CI hardware to enable win_arm64 wheels for PyArrow, conditioned on abi3 consolidation. [Thread](https://lists.apache.org/list?dev@arrow.apache.org:lte=2M:DISCUSS%20Python%20CI%20win_arm64%20support%20for%20PyArrow%20proposed%20plan%20and%20commitment)

* **Apache Arrow 25.0.1 patch release:** Maintenance release resolving 9 issues, including performance improvements for `to_pylist()` on nested types in PyArrow. [Thread](https://lists.apache.org/list?dev@arrow.apache.org:lte=2M:VOTE%20Release%20Apache%20Arrow%2025.0.1%20-%20RC1)

* **Canonical BigDecimal extension type:** Discussion on a new `arrow.big_decimal` type for variable-scale numeric data, though some maintainers expressed concerns regarding its current representation. [Thread](https://lists.apache.org/list?dev@arrow.apache.org:lte=2M:VOTE%20Format%20Proposal%20for%20Canonical%20BigDecimal%20Extension%20Type)

* **Limits on concurrent open PRs:** Proposal to limit non-committers to three concurrent open Pull Requests to mitigate an influx of stale or potentially AI-generated contributions. [Thread](https://lists.apache.org/list?dev@arrow.apache.org:lte=2M:DISCUSS%20Limit%20on%20concurrent%20open%20PRs%20for%20non-committers)

* **Variant and Parquet spec alignment:** Technical discussion on explicitly disallowing certain types (like unsigned integers) in the Variant Arrow representation to maintain consistency with Parquet shredding. [Thread](https://lists.apache.org/list?dev@arrow.apache.org:lte=2M:DISCUSS%20Format%20Variant%20extension%20spec%20inconsistent%20with%20the%20Parquet%20shredding%20spec)

* **Introduction of ArxLang:** Announcement of Arx, an experimental data-oriented programming language designed with Apache Arrow as its native foundation for arrays and DataFrames. [Thread](https://lists.apache.org/list?dev@arrow.apache.org:lte=2M:DISCUSS%20ArxLang%20Arrow-native%20data%20types%20in%20a%20programming%20language)
