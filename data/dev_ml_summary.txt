* **Arrow 26.0.0 release candidate:** Voting is underway for the major 26.0.0 release, featuring over 300 resolved issues and expanded testing across various OS distributions. [Thread](https://lists.apache.org/list?dev@arrow.apache.org:lte=2M:VOTE%20Release%20Apache%20Arrow%2026.0.0%20-%20RC0)

* **Arrow Rust 60.0.0 release:** A major update to the Rust implementation including new Parquet encoding support (ALP), significant kernel performance improvements, and work to reduce panics in the codebase. [Thread](https://lists.apache.org/list?dev@arrow.apache.org:lte=2M:ANNOUCNCE%20Arrow%20Rust%2060.0.0)

* **Splitting PyArrow into modular wheels:** Discussion on breaking down the monolithic PyArrow installation into separate wheels (e.g., `pyarrow-s3`) to reduce binary sizes and better manage heavy dependencies. [Thread](https://lists.apache.org/list?dev@arrow.apache.org:lte=2M:DISCUSS%20Improve%20modularity%20of%20PyArrow)

* **Deprecation of Tensor and SparseTensor in IPC:** The community voted to formally deprecate Tensor and SparseTensor types in the IPC format to simplify the specification and focus on core columnar data. [Thread](https://lists.apache.org/list?dev@arrow.apache.org:lte=2M:RESULTVOTE%20Informal%20deprecation%20of%20Tensor%20and%20SparseTensor%20in%20IPC)

* **Dropping macOS Intel support:** Proposal to discontinue official support and CI testing for x86_64 Mac hardware following Homebrew's deprecation and the shift toward Apple silicon. [Thread](https://lists.apache.org/list?dev@arrow.apache.org:lte=2M:DISCUSS%20Drop%20macOS%20Intel%20support)

* **New guidelines for AI-generated contributions:** Proposal to establish reviewer guidelines for handling low-quality AI PRs, including a new "needs-author-engagement" label to flag lack of author clarity. [Thread](https://lists.apache.org/list?dev@arrow.apache.org:lte=2M:DISCUSS%20AI-generated%20contributions)

* **Canonical extension types for bounded ranges:** Voting has opened for `arrow.fixed_closedness_range` and `arrow.variable_closedness_range` to standardize how mathematical intervals are represented across implementations. [Thread](https://lists.apache.org/list?dev@arrow.apache.org:lte=2M:VOTEFormat%20Fixed%20and%20variable%20closedness%20range%20canonical%20extension%20types)

* **JSON representation of Arrow schemas:** Ongoing efforts to standardize a JSON-based format for schemas to improve data contract definitions and facilitate cross-process communication. [Thread](https://lists.apache.org/list?dev@arrow.apache.org:lte=2M:DISCUSSFormat%20JSON%20representation%20of%20Arrow%20schemas)

* **ArrowMetal 0.1.0 announcement:** Introduction of a new project enabling Arrow compute on Apple silicon GPUs via the Metal framework, potentially revitalizing the Device Data interface. [Thread](https://lists.apache.org/list?dev@arrow.apache.org:lte=2M:ANNOUNCE%20ArrowMetal%200.1.0%20Arrow%20compute%20on%20Apple%20silicon%20GPUs%20through%20Metal)
