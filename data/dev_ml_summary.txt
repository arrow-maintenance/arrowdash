* **Nullability semantics in nested types:** Discussion on clarifying the IPC format's `nullable` flag for complex types like nested structs and unions to ensure consistent behavior across C++, Python, and Go. [Thread](https://lists.apache.org/list?dev@arrow.apache.org:lte=2M:Semantics%20of%20non-nullable%20fields%20with%20non-trivial%20types)

* **Canonical BigDecimal extension type:** Formal vote to adopt `arrow.big_decimal` as a canonical extension type, allowing for variable-scale numeric data often found in databases like PostgreSQL and Oracle. [Thread](https://lists.apache.org/list?dev@arrow.apache.org:lte=2M:Proposal%20for%20Canonical%20BigDecimal%20Extension%20Type)

* **Windows ARM64 support for PyArrow:** Microsoft proposed a detailed support plan for `win_arm64` wheels, offering dedicated engineering time for build consolidation and hardware for maintainers. [Thread](https://lists.apache.org/list?dev@arrow.apache.org:lte=2M:win_arm64%20support%20for%20PyArrow%20proposed%20plan%20and%20commitment)

* **Inconsistency in Variant extension spec:** Developers identified a discrepancy between the Arrow Variant extension and the Parquet shredding spec regarding unsigned integers, proposing a refresh to ensure Parquet round-trip compatibility. [Thread](https://lists.apache.org/list?dev@arrow.apache.org:lte=2M:Variant%20extension%20spec%20inconsistent%20with%20the%20Parquet%20shredding%20spec)

* **Pull request limits for non-committers:** Proposal to limit the number of concurrent open PRs for contributors without write access to manage the influx of stale or low-quality AI-generated contributions. [Thread](https://lists.apache.org/list?dev@arrow.apache.org:lte=2M:Limit%20on%20concurrent%20open%20PRs%20for%20non-committers)

* **Swift 6.0 upgrade proposal:** Discussion on raising the minimum Swift version to 6.0 to leverage native `Int128` support, which is essential for high-precision decimal arithmetic in the Swift implementation. [Thread](https://lists.apache.org/list?dev@arrow.apache.org:lte=2M:Proposal%20to%20raise%20minimum%20Swift%20version%20to%206.0)

* **Major releases (Arrow 25.0.0 and ADBC 24):** Announcement of the 25.0.0 core release featuring over 200 resolved issues, alongside the release of ADBC 24 and maintenance updates for Go, Rust, and JavaScript. [Thread](https://lists.apache.org/list?dev@arrow.apache.org:lte=2M:ANNOUNCE%20Apache%20Arrow%2025.0.0%20released)

* **New maintainer appointments:** The project announced Jeffrey Vo as a new PMC member and Tadeja Kadunc as a new committer, recognizing their significant contributions to the Rust implementation and CI stability. [Thread](https://lists.apache.org/list?dev@arrow.apache.org:lte=2M:ANNOUNCE%20New%20Arrow%20PMC%20member%20Jeffrey%20Vo%20Jefffrey)
